# OpenWALDO Public Corpus Index

Open source communities built much of the modern technological world by giving
people and organizations a common foundation they could inspect, improve, teach,
and build upon together. OpenWALDO brings that proven model to AI training data.
This repository is the shared public index: a place where individuals,
researchers, institutions, and companies can collaborate on the foundational
corpus work that benefits every model built above it.

Instead of every team repeatedly assembling the same foundational material, the
community can build one growing, accountable record of sources, licenses, and
corpus history. Every contribution compounds: a source added once can support
many models, a correction strengthens the public record, and better tooling
helps every future contributor. Builders remain free to select the material
that fits their work, create their own models, and differentiate above the
shared foundation.

The index makes that collaboration inspectable. Git-governed metadata records
corpus meaning, source and license assertions, counts, and content hashes, while
canonical data lives in content-addressed lookaside storage. Resolved selections
become immutable Bills of Materials that can follow the data through training,
model creation, and release. Public review, attributable contributions, and DCO
sign-off keep accountability attached as the corpus grows—creating a foundation
the entire open source AI community can verify, improve, and use.
